## html-ppt-skill

K Teach's self-contained presentation runtime was informed by the interaction
model of `html-ppt-skill`, pinned at commit
`f3a8435d3901697d5ac5e64d356c933637e43107`. K Teach implements its own
Presentation Brief, renderer, layouts, styling, presenter query mode, notes,
timer, overview, keyboard navigation, print rules, and reduced-motion rules.
It does not require the upstream Skill or copy its gallery, themes, fonts,
effects, or runtime assets.
